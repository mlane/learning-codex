## While studying

- **Slow down for:** "pick X when", "prefer X over Y", exam tips, zonal versus regional
  versus global, anything that surprised you.
- **Speed up for:** console tours, feature lists, product history.
- **During:** one-line signals in `inbox.md`. Stay out of the cards.
- **After:** ten minutes, inbox to cards, three cards at most, from memory.
- **New card only if:** it came up in a lecture or lab, is named in a case study, is in
  a question you missed, or is medium depth or above on the trainer's sheet.
